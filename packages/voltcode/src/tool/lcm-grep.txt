- Fast regex search through earlier conversation history
- Searches messages that have been summarized and are no longer in current context
- Supports full regex syntax (PostgreSQL regex ~)
- Results are grouped by their covering summary ID
- Returns matching messages with seq number, role, and content snippet
- Paginated at ~40KB per page for large result sets
- Use this when you need to find specific content from earlier in the conversation
- Does NOT spawn a sub-agent - results are injected directly into context
